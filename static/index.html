In Sales Navigator open a lead list or search results page, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste here. Extra text such as “Saved Badge”, “2nd”, “Add note” or dates is removed automatically. Paste one page at a time: each paste is added to your sheet and duplicates are skipped. You can also paste columns from Excel or Google Sheets – e.g. Company · Name · Title (with or without a header row, an Email column is kept) – and the tool works out which column is which.

Find new people with filters, like Sales Navigator. Every filter value is combined with every other into separate Google searches, e.g. site:linkedin.com/in/ "Head of Sales" "Zoho" "Chennai". Each profile found is checked against your filters. Google shows at most ~100 results per search, so more designations, companies or regions give more leads. All boxes are optional – fill in what you need.
