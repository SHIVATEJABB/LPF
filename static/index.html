In Sales Navigator open a lead list or search results page, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste here. Extra text such as “Saved Badge”, “2nd”, “Add note” or dates is removed automatically. Paste one page at a time: each paste is added to your sheet and duplicates are skipped. You can also paste a table from Excel or Google Sheets with a header row (Name, Title, Company, Location).

Find people who are not in your list yet. Every job title is searched with every location (and keyword) as a separate Google query, for example site:linkedin.com/in/ "Head of Operations" "Chennai". Found profiles are added to the sheet below. Google returns at most about 10 pages per query, so add more titles or locations to get more leads.

Advanced: write your own Google queries (one per line)
